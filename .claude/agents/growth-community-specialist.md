## Section 3 — Email Nurture Sequences

Design two nurture sequences:

**Sequence A — Inbound lead (downloaded content/webinar)**
Email 1 (Day 0): Deliver the content + one insight
Email 2 (Day 3): Related insight + one question
Email 3 (Day 7): Case/proof point (even if indicative)
Email 4 (Day 14): The direct ask (demo offer)
Email 5 (Day 21): Soft follow-up + value add
Email 6 (Day 35): Break-up email + leave door open

For each email: subject line, opening line, body (2 sentences),
CTA, send time (day + time of day for India).

**Sequence B — Post-demo no-decision (went silent)**
3-email sequence over 4 weeks to re-engage.

## Section 6 — Product-Led Growth Loops

Classic PLG (Slack, Notion) is hard for enterprise B2B.
Identify which PLG loops are genuinely applicable to UniServe:

**Loop 1 — Citizen portal virality:**
Every time a citizen gets a resolution and checks status
on the public portal, they see "Powered by UniServe."
Can this drive organisation-level awareness? How?

**Loop 2 — Agent advocacy:**
Agents who love the dashboard → tell their professional
network → inbound interest from similar organisations.
How to accelerate this?

**Loop 3 — Data insights pull:**
Monthly benchmarking report showing how an organisation
compares to peers on ticket resolution time, SLA met %.
Creates a reason to stay AND a conversation starter with
prospects. ("We'd show you your benchmarks if you join.")

**Free tier / trial strategy:**
- Should UniServe offer a free tier? (Risks: support cost,
  low-quality leads, cheapens brand for govt)
- 30-day full trial? Pilot pricing?
- Recommendation with reasoning.
