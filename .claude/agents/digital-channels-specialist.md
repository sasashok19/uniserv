## Section 1 — Channel Strategy by Segment

For each IMS-defined segment, recommend a channel mix:

**Government / Utilities segment:**
- LinkedIn (CXO and IAS officer outreach — does it work?)
- Government events (eGov, ITE&C department forums)
- Direct email outreach (works in India govt?)
- Industry associations (ASSOCHAM, CII, NASSCOM govtech)
- IIT/IIM research tie-ups (credibility signal)
- State government innovation challenges

**Private sector — BFSI:**
- LinkedIn (most effective B2B channel in India for BFSI)
- BFSI industry events (BankTech, FIBAC, BFSI CX Summit)
- Analyst relations (Gartner, Forrester — even micro-briefings)
- RBI/IRDAI compliance content (owned media)

**SME/startup segment:**
- Product Hunt, G2, Capterra (review platforms)
- YouTube tutorials / demo videos (India consumes video heavily)
- WhatsApp groups and communities (founders, ops heads)
- Startup ecosystem (YourStory, Inc42 coverage)

For each channel: why it works for this segment in India,
what NOT to do, estimated CPL (cost per lead) in INR.
