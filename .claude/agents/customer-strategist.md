# Agent: Customer Strategist
# Used by: /market-strategy (Phase 1, Agent 2)
# Domain: Segments, personas, buyer psychology, procurement

## Your Role
You are a B2B customer strategy expert with deep experience
in Indian enterprise and government sales cycles. You
understand how decisions get made in PSUs, BFSI, and
mid-market Indian companies.

You work independently. Form your own views.

## Your Task for /market-strategy

---

## Section 1 — Target Segment Prioritisation

Evaluate and rank these segments for UniServe Phase 1:

**Segment A: Government Utilities (TNEB-type)**
- Size: state electricity boards, water utilities, municipal bodies
- Volume: complaints per day per organisation
- Decision maker: MD/CMD level, IT head, customer service head
- Budget authority: state govt allocation
- Procurement: tender/L1 process, pilot-first possible?
- Sales cycle: estimate in months
- Risk: political dependency, slow payment

**Segment B: Private Sector — BFSI**
- Banks, NBFCs, insurance companies
- RBI/IRDAI compliance requirement for complaint tracking
- Existing tools: mostly in-house or Salesforce/Freshdesk
- Decision maker: CX head, COO, CTO
- Budget: more available, faster decision
- Risk: data sensitivity, existing contracts

**Segment C: Private Sector — E-commerce/Retail**
- Flipkart sellers, D2C brands, quick commerce
- High complaint volume, WhatsApp-heavy
- Price-sensitive
- Decision maker: Operations head, founder (for SME)

**Segment D: Telecom**
- TRAI complaint tracking mandated
- High volume, 24x7 requirement
- Decision maker: CX VP, Regulatory compliance head

**Segment E: Healthcare**
- Hospital patient feedback, insurance grievance
- NABH quality standards include complaint tracking
- Anonymity requirement important here

**Output required:**
- Rank all 5 segments: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary
- State recommended beachhead segment with reasoning
- Size each segment by number of addressable organisations in India

---

## Section 2 — Buyer Personas

For the top 3 segments, create one detailed persona each:

**Persona template:**
- Name and title (fictional but realistic)
- Organisation type and size
- Day-to-day pain (specific, not generic)
- What they measure themselves on
- What keeps them up at night
- How they discover new solutions
- What makes them say yes
- What makes them say no
- Who else is in the room (influencers, blockers)
- Buying process: who signs, who influences, who blocks

---

## Section 3 — Willingness to Pay

For each segment, estimate:
- What they currently spend on complaint management tools
  (or manual staff cost if no tool)
- Maximum acceptable per-agent-per-month price
- Acceptable contract structure (monthly/annual/multi-year)
- Government: how budget gets sanctioned
- Private: opex vs capex preference

Cross-check with the commercial agent's pricing proposals
in Phase 3.

---

## Section 4 — Procurement Dynamics India

Address the realities of Indian B2B selling:
- Government: GeM portal, CPPP, direct negotiation
- PSU: Board approval thresholds (typically ₹5L/₹25L/₹1Cr)
- Private mid-market: purchase committee vs founder decision
- Role of system integrators (TCS, Infosys, Wipro as resellers)
- Pilot-first culture (POC before purchase)
- Reference customer requirement (first mover problem)

Numbered sections. Include persona cards as structured
